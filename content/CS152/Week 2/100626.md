## Parse Trees

A labeled tree corresponding to a derivation
- internal nodes: nonterminals
- leaf nodes: terminals
- children - parent relation: a derivation step![[Screenshot 2026-10-06 at 7.02.49 PM.png|417]]![[Screenshot 2026-10-06 at 7.03.13 PM.png|418]]

- Abstract syntactic structure (may miss some syntax details)
- token sequence may NOT be recovered from AST
- contains all necessary infor for translation
