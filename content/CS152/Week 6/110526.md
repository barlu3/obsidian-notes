## Symbol Tables

A data structure for storing attributes for "symbols"
- declaration information: type and scope
- may be involved during parsing (or lexing) but better to be constructed during semantic analysis
- operations: insert, lookup, delete
![[Pasted image 20261105185358.png]]

Can be implemented as a dictionary
- Linear list, Tree (BST, AVL tree, B-tree)
- Hash table
	- problem of collision but its actually a good thing 
Collisions allow us to understand the scope of the symbol that we have in the program. When there's a collision, the newest variable at the hashed index is most-left while older, out-of-scope variables are to the right. 
- How do we know the variable becomes out of scope? When do we remove it?
	- At each new scope, create a new hash table
