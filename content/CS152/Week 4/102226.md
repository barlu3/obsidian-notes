## LR(0) conflicts

Shift-reduce
$$
\begin{align}
E' \to E.  \\
E \to E. + n
\end{align}
$$
Reduce-reduce
$$
\begin{align}
E' \to E. \\
E \to E + n.
\end{align}
$$
How to resolve 
- If item $\ne$ complete and FIRST(+) $==$ lookahead then use this item
- If item $=$ complete reduce only when lookahead $==$ Follow($E'$)
