## LR(0) conflicts

Shift-reduce
$$
\begin{align}
E' \to E.  \\
E \to E. + n
\end{align}
$$
Reduce-reduce
$$
\begin{align}
E' \to E. \\
E \to E + n.
\end{align}
$$
How to resolve 
- If item $\ne$ complete and FIRST(+) $==$ lookahead then use this item
- If item $=$ complete reduce only when lookahead $==$ Follow($E'$)

## SLR(1) Grammar

A grammar is SLR(1) if and only if, for any state s, we have 
- any two items $A \to \alpha. \beta$ and $B \to \gamma$
	- First($\beta$) $\cap$ Follow(B) = $\emptyset$
- any two items $A\to \alpha.$ and $B \to \gamma$
	- Follow(A) $\cap$ Follow(B) = $\emptyset$ 

## LR(1)

a.k.a Canonical LR(1)
- LR(1) builds lookahead into DFA - LR(1) DFA
	- use its context to find expected next tokens
	- LR(1) item: $[ A\to \alpha. B, \space a]$ 
		- $A \to \alpha. B$ is an LR(0) item
		- $a$ is the expected next token after $\alpha \beta$ is reduced to $A$
