## Bottom-Up Parsing

Given a string, reduce that string into its respective derivation rules until you get to the starting derivation rule. 

- Shift a terminal from the front of the input to the stack top
- Reduce the stack top $\alpha$ to A, based on the rule $A \to \alpha$

### Some Terminology

*Right Sentential Form (RSF)*: intermediate strings of terminals and nonterminal from rightmost derivation
![[Screenshot 2026-10-20 at 6.55.17 PM.png|68]]![[Screenshot 2026-10-20 at 6.59.53 PM.png|123]]
- Handle of RSF: (i) a substring in RSF (including its position) and (ii) the production rule used to reduce it
*LR Parser*: L means left to right scanning, R means rightmost derivation in reverse
- Task in LR parsing: determining the handle, thus the next RSF

### LR(0) Items

*Item*: a state representing the status of RHS recognition
- use a dot "." to separate the recognized part and the rest
LR(0) means no need to look ahead to determine handle![[Screenshot 2026-10-20 at 7.07.34 PM.png]]

- Everything LHS of the dot "." means it *is* in the parsing stack, everything RHS of the dot "." means it *is not* in the parsing stack 

Use Finite Automata to connect items to track the progress of RHS recognition
