## Bottom-Up Parsing

Given a string, reduce that string into its respective derivation rules until you get to the starting derivation rule. 

- Shift a terminal from the front of the input to the stack top
- Reduce the stack top $\alpha$ to A, based on the rule $A \to \alpha$

### Some Terminology

*Right Sentential Form (RSF)*: intermediate strings of terminals and nonterminal from rightmost derivation
![[Screenshot 2026-10-20 at 6.55.17 PM.png|68]]
- Handle of RSF: (i) a substring in RSF (including its position) and (ii) the production rule used to reduce it
*LR Parser*: L means left to right scanning, R means rightmost derivation in reverse
- Task in LR parsing: determining the handle, thust he next RSF
